## Design Context

### Users
Teams working through Claude Code, Codex and Cursor; the current presentation is for hackathon judges. The product makes decisions, evidence and agent handoffs available through shared memory.

### Brand Personality
Connected, precise, bold. The user requested striking hackathon copy and a distinctive CSS/SVG logo with custom Strata Sync typography.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refine the original four gently tilted blue/cyan/green/blue strata on a navy rounded tile. Flat fills, consistent spacing, no glow or ghost stripes. Pair with the existing custom vector wordmark. The eight alternative concepts remain archived for comparison. No font dependency for the logo.

### Design Principles
- One recognizable mark at both favicon and presentation sizes.
- Express layers and continuity through geometry.
- Keep lettering readable and let the product story carry the personality.
- Use shared SVG assets across app, manual and presentation materials.
